Nova Merge: Planet Puzzle

Support and mobile app privacy policy · Updated September 11, 2026

Developer: Slop Games / Jang Yunsoo (장윤수), Republic of Korea.
Contact: support@slop-games.com

Scope

This policy covers the Nova Merge mobile app, including Android package com.novamerge.game and the iOS app. The Slop Games website has a separate policy. The game does not require a Nova Merge account and has no advertising or in-app purchases in version 1.17.0.

Progress on your device

Scores, discovered worlds, game progress and sound, music, haptic and other preferences are stored locally. We do not operate a server that receives this local save. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on its settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the active local save; restored backups may restore it.

Platform game services

The Android app includes the Google Play Games Services SDK. Google describes SDK analytics and diagnostics used for stability and product improvement. Where game-service sign-in and leaderboards are configured and available, Google may process your game profile and submitted scores. The Android leaderboard is not configured in version 1.17.0; the game does not submit scores through that feature.

On iOS, optional Apple Game Center features use your Apple-managed game profile and submitted scores when available and signed in. The developer does not receive your Google or Apple password. Game-service profile visibility is controlled by the platform and your settings. Service providers may process data outside your country under their own policies.

See Google Play Games data practices, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple Game Center privacy. Google describes its game-service data transfers as encrypted with HTTPS.

Your choices and deletion

You can play the core game without a Nova Merge account. Manage or delete Google game data in your Play Games profile or Google Account settings. Manage Game Center and related data through your Apple account and device settings. These providers control their service records and retention. For questions or requests about information you send directly to us, email support@slop-games.com.

Support messages

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and address the reported issue. Do not send passwords. We retain correspondence while needed for support or applicable obligations, then delete it. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for advertising.

Audience and updates

The app is intended for general players aged 13 and over, and is not directed to children under 13. It has no chat or user-generated posts. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change.
